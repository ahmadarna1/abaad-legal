Privacy Policy

abaad · Effective October 10, 2026

abaad ("the app") is a private analytics tool operated by the owner of the abaad academy TikTok account. It is used only by that owner to produce weekly performance reports about the account's own content. It is not offered to the public or to other TikTok users.

What we access

With the account owner's permission through TikTok Login Kit, the app uses the TikTok Display API to read:

  • Basic profile information: display name and profile picture (user.info.basic).
  • Profile details: bio and profile link (user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • The account's own public videos with their titles, posting dates, durations, and view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).

The app does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok, does not read private messages, and does not access any other user's account.

How we use it

The data is used only to calculate weekly growth, rank videos, identify the best posting times, and display the results in a private report and dashboard for the account owner.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored in the owner's own automation workflow and shown on a private dashboard protected by a secret key.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. Summaries of the numbers may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic) solely to generate written insights for the owner.
  • Weekly snapshots are kept for up to one year, then deleted.

Your choices

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage apps (the exact menu names may vary by app version). On request we will delete all stored data.
